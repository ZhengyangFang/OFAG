# Development records

The [Stillwater case](stillwater.md) predates the three field cases in the TLE
manuscript. It remains available because the agent lesson corpus cites
observations from it and its quoted evidence is checked against this record.
It is not a fourth manuscript case or a source for the manuscript figures.

The [plugin contract history](plugin_contract_evolution.md),
[seismic engine survey](seismic_engine_selection.md), and
[Soda Lake field record](seismic_field_data.md) document implementation choices
and a field dataset outside the three manuscript cases. They remain available
for provenance and for the agent's development knowledge base.

The [exploratory benchmark](benchmark/) contains historical task definitions
and a summary of its evaluation limits. Its detailed run reports are retained
locally by the author. It is separate from the three field case results in the
manuscript.

The full [FORGE](case1_forge_history.md),
[Cedar Rapids](case2_cedar_rapids_history.md), and
[Llano](case3_llano_history.md) case histories retain development-time
diagnoses and decisions. Their paper-facing summaries remain at the original
case-note paths.

The current paper cases and their execution stages are listed in the
[reproduction guide](../reproducibility.md). Historical observations should
not be counted as discoveries by agents working on the final cases.
